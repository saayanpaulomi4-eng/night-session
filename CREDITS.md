# Credits

## Player bodies
- Built from **MakeHuman** data (https://www.makehumancommunity.org, github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman): the base human mesh, body-shape and face targets, the default skeleton and its skin weights, and the low-poly eyes. These assets are released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
- In this game, the body is retargeted onto the game's own skeleton. The hands are curled into a racket grip, the feet are removed (the game's shoes go there), and clothing, brows and stubble are shaded by the game.
- The faces are generic. They are not likenesses of any real person.

## Sound
- Racket/ball impact: "Tennis-Ball-Hit" by kletton97, https://freesound.org/people/kletton97/sounds/710041/, CC0 1.0 (public domain). Every shot type is shaped from this recording in the game.

Everything else (crowd, bounces, net, music-free ambience, visuals and code) is generated by the game itself.
